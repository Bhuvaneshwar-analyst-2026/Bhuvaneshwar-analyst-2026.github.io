I am a Business Analyst, Data Analyst, and Financial Analyst with a Master of Business Analytics from the University of Dayton and a Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com) in Finance and Accounting from Ballari Institute of Technology & Management (BITM)

My academic background combines business, finance, and analytics, allowing me to understand both the numbers behind business performance and the analytical methods used to drive strategic decisions. I have developed expertise in SQL, Python, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, Business Intelligence, Data Visualization, Forecasting, KPI Reporting, and Data Analytics.

I am passionate about transforming complex data into actionable business insights, improving business processes, and helping organizations make informed decisions through analytics and strategic thinking.
